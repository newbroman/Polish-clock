🎮 Interaction

  • Dragging: Move the outer ring for minutes, inner circle for hours.
  • Manual: Type directly into the box (e.g., 16:45).

👔 Formal Logic (24h)

Used for schedules and official announcements.

Structure: Godzina + Ordinal Hour + Cardinal Minute.

  • 14:10 — Godzina czternasta dziesięć
  • 08:05 — Godzina ósma zero pięć

☕ Casual Grammar (12h)

In casual speech, the Hour changes its ending based on the preposition used.

Time Context Case Used Example
On the hour Nominative Pierwsza (1:00)
Po (Past) Genitive Pięć po pierwszej (1:05)
Wpół do (Half to) Genitive Wpół do drugiej (1:30)
Za (To) Nominative Za pięć druga (1:55)
