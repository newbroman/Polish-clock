📱 How to Use

  • Drag the Hands: Move the clock hands to change the time.
  • Manual Entry: Type a time (e.g., 14:20) in the box.
  • Formal/Casual Toggle: Switch between official 24h time and daily 12h time.
  • EN/PL: Switch the interface language.
  • Quiz Mode: Pick the correct Polish phrase. Green is correct, Red is wrong.

☕ Casual Grammar (12h)

In casual speech, the Minute is always Cardinal. The Hour is always Ordinal, but its case depends on the preposition used:

Structure Hour Case Example
On the Hour Nominative Czwarta (4:00)
Po (Past) Genitive pięć po czwartej (4:05)
Wpół do (Half) Genitive wpół do piątej (4:30)
Za (To) Nominative za pięć piąta (4:55)
