📱 How to Use

  • Drag the Hands: Move the clock hands to change the time.
  • Manual Entry: Type a time (e.g., 14:20) in the box.
  • Formal/Casual Toggle: Switch between official 24h and daily 12h time.
  • Quiz Mode: Test yourself! Pick the correct Polish phrase. Green for correct, Red for wrong.

👔 Formal Grammar (24h)

Used for official schedules. Hour is Ordinal, Minute is Cardinal.

Time Structure Example
17:10 Hour (Nom.) + Minute siedemnasta dziesięć

☕ Casual Grammar (12h)

The Hour case changes based on the preposition used:

Structure Preposition Case Example
On the Hour Nominative Czwarta (4:00)
Past po (+ Genitive) pięć po czwartej
Half past wpół do (+ Genitive) wpół do piątej
To za (+ Nominative) za dziesięć piąta

Note on Minutes & Seconds: These are always cardinal (Blue) numbers in both formal and casual forms. After 23, they continue their standard form (e.g., 24 is dwadzieścia cztery). Seconds follow the minutes directly (e.g., 12:05:10 is dwunasta pięć dziesięć).

Note on Hours: In 👔 Formal mode, always use the Nominative Ordinal (Orange). In ☕ Casual mode, use Genitive (Purple) after po and wpół do, but use Nominative (Orange) for full hours and after za.

Special Terms: For 12:00, you can use południe (Noon) and for 00:00, północ (Midnight).
