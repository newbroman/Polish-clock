📱 How to Use

  • Drag the Hands: Change time by moving the clock hands.
  • Manual Entry: Type time directly (e.g., 16:45).
  • Formal/Casual Toggle: Switch between 24h and 12h phrasing.
  • Quiz Mode: Test your knowledge! Green = Correct.

👔 Formal Grammar (24h)

Used for schedules. Hour is Ordinal, Minute is Cardinal.

Time Structure Example
17:10 Hour (Nom.) + Minute siedemnasta dziesięć

☕ Casual Grammar (12h)

The Hour case changes based on the preposition used:

Structure Preposition Case Example
On the Hour Nominative Czwarta (4:00)
Past po (+ Genitive) pięć po czwartej
Half past wpół do (+ Genitive) wpół do piątej
To za (+ Nominative) za dziesięć piąta
